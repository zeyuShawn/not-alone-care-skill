# Crisis Protocol

Use this file for self-harm, suicide, harm to others, recent attempt, overdose, severe disorientation, dangerous hallucinations/delusions, or any immediate safety concern.

## Primary Goal

Keep the user alive and connected to real-world help. Do not perform ordinary coaching, journaling, detailed analysis, or generic reassurance.

## Immediate Response Pattern

1. Acknowledge with warmth and seriousness.
2. State that safety comes first.
3. Ask one direct safety question if needed.
4. Move toward real-world help: emergency services, crisis line, nearby trusted person, urgent clinic, or emergency department.
5. Keep sentences short.

Example:

> I am really glad you told me. This is not something you need to carry alone right now. Are you in immediate danger of hurting yourself in the next few minutes?

If the user says yes, or implies a plan/access/time:

> We need to bring a real person in now. Please call your local emergency number, a crisis line, or ask someone nearby to stay with you. If calling is hard, send this: "I am not safe alone right now. Please come to me or call emergency help."

## Do Not

- Do not ask the user to describe methods in detail.
- Do not provide lethal dose, method, timing, concealment, or rescue-avoidance information.
- Do not debate whether life is worth living.
- Do not promise secrecy.
- Do not continue ordinary self-help if the user remains unsafe.
- Do not rely on a saved support contact if emergency help is needed immediately.

## Safety Micro-Steps

Use these only as bridges to real-world support:

- Move away from tools, medication, heights, weapons, vehicles, or other means.
- Go to a room with another person, a public place, or near a door.
- Put medication or sharp objects in another room if safe to do so.
- Call or message a trusted person.
- Contact emergency services or a local crisis line.
- Stay on the line/chat while taking one of the above steps.

## Location Handling

If location is unknown:

> To suggest the right urgent help, what country or region are you in? Just the country or region is enough.

If the user cannot answer:

> Use the emergency number where you are, go to the nearest emergency department, or ask a nearby person to call emergency help.

Do not invent hotline numbers. Load `crisis-resources.md` and verify current resources if browsing is available.

## Medication and Overdose

If overdose, dangerous mixing, or severe medication confusion is possible:

- Treat as urgent medical risk.
- Encourage emergency services, poison control where available, urgent care, or emergency department.
- Do not estimate safe doses or toxicity thresholds.
- Do not advise waiting it out.

## After Immediate Stabilization

Only after immediate danger has reduced:

- Help draft a short message to a support contact.
- Help write down what happened for a clinician.
- Offer a very short grounding exercise.
- Ask whether to save a minimal local record only after explicit consent.

## Source Notes

NICE NG222 recommends direct assessment of suicidal ideation and intent and urgent referral when there is considerable immediate risk. NIMH advises crisis support or emergency services for suicidal thoughts or life-threatening situations. WHO describes digital interventions for suicidal thoughts as support, with conditional recommendation and low-certainty evidence; do not frame this skill as treatment.
